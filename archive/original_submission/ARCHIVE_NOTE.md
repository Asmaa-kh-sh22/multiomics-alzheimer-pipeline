# Original submission (archived, do not use)

These files are the code and results from the first submission. They are kept only for transparency.

They contain two errors:

1. GWAS positions (GRCh37) were matched with gene positions from GRCh38.
2. The probe annotation accepted cytogenetic locations as gene symbols, and the overlap background was taken from the whole platform annotation.

The results in these files (the 8 candidate genes, the hypergeometric p = 0.048, and the enrichment results) are not valid.

Use `revision_analysis.py` in the main folder.
